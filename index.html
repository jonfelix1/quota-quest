How the allocator decides

Done reports claim their quota first, exactly as photographed, and are never re-solved. Remaining sessions are then planned oldest-first. Eligible funders are attendees with quarter budget and a report slot left (plus, in strict mode, no report already filed that month). The report is seeded with the highest-headroom candidates, forced to span two departments, then cost splits evenly capped by each person's remaining quota. Surplus re-spreads and extra funders join until covered. If quota cannot reach the minimum number of paying names, the session is reported unfunded rather than filed short-staffed. Greedy: good plans, not provably optimal. Quarters never share budget.
